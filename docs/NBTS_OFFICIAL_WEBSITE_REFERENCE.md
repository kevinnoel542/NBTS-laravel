# NBTS Official Website Reference

This file explains what the official NBTS Tanzania website contains and what public pages our NBTS system should support.

Source site checked: https://www.nbts.go.tz/

The goal is not to copy the old website design. The goal is to understand the important content and build our own cleaner, modern pages with the same public information needs.

## Main Website Purpose

The public website should help visitors do these things:

- Understand what NBTS is.
- Learn why blood donation matters.
- Know if they are allowed to donate.
- Find where to donate blood.
- See upcoming campaigns and events.
- Read news and public announcements.
- Learn about NBTS services.
- Contact NBTS.
- Download or use the donor mobile app.

## Services Page

The official site has these service pages:

- Blood collection.
- Laboratory.
- Proper use of blood.
- Quality management system.

Our Services page should explain each service clearly.

### Blood Collection

This section should explain:

- NBTS collects blood from voluntary unpaid donors.
- There are two main donation types.
- Whole blood donation.
- Apheresis donation, where only selected blood components are collected.

Donation products mentioned by the official site:

- Platelets.
- Plasma.
- Red blood cells.

### Laboratory Services

This section should explain:

- NBTS laboratories test collected blood.
- The laboratories are in regional zones.
- Each blood unit is tested for major transfusion-transmitted infections.
- Blood group testing is done using ABO and Rh systems.
- Extra tests can be done for safety.
- Blood can be processed into different blood products.
- Blood and blood products are stored using proper cold chain equipment.
- Safe blood is supplied to registered health facilities.

Important tests mentioned by the official site:

- HIV.
- Hepatitis B.
- Hepatitis C.
- Syphilis.
- ABO blood group.
- Rh blood group.

### Proper Use Of Blood

This section should explain:

- NBTS supports health facilities with guidance and education.
- The goal is correct and safe use of blood and blood products.
- NBTS helps health workers understand transfusion practice and available blood products.

### Quality Management

This section should explain:

- NBTS uses a quality management system across its work.
- Quality starts from donor recruitment and continues to patient care after transfusion.
- NBTS participates in proficiency testing.
- NBTS supports training and quality improvement.
- NBTS monitors quality of blood products.

## Why Donate Blood

The official site explains that Tanzania still needs more blood collections compared to estimated need.

This page should explain:

- Blood is needed for emergencies and serious medical conditions.
- Donated blood helps pregnant mothers, children, accident victims, cancer patients, surgery patients, and other patients.
- Blood cannot be replaced by a machine or manufactured like ordinary medicine.
- A healthy eligible person can save lives by donating.

Recommended message:

- Donate blood. Save lives.

## Who Can Donate

The official site gives donor eligibility requirements.

Our public eligibility page should include:

- Age must be 18 to 65 years.
- Weight should be at least 50 kg.
- Hemoglobin should be at least 12.5 g/dL.
- Donor should be in good health.
- Donor should not have conditions or infections that make donation unsafe.
- Men can donate after about 3 months from the last donation.
- Women can donate after about 4 months from the last donation.

The page should also link to:

- Public eligibility checker.
- Mobile app profile completion.
- Booking page or app download.

## Reasons Someone May Not Donate

The official site lists deferral reasons.

Our page should explain:

- Some people are deferred for a short time.
- Some people are deferred permanently.
- Deferral protects both donor and patient.

Common reasons:

- Age below 18 or above 65.
- Weight below 50 kg.
- Low hemoglobin.
- Not feeling well.
- Long-term illness that makes donation unsafe.
- Known blood-transmitted infection.
- Donated too recently.
- Some medicines can temporarily stop donation.

Important note:

- This public page gives general guidance only. Staff or health workers make the final decision.

## Donation Process Page

The official site gives a seven-step donation process.

Our page should show the process like this:

1. Donor arrives at reception.
2. Donor is registered.
3. Donor weight is checked.
4. Donor hemoglobin level is checked.
5. Donor answers health questions.
6. Eligible donor gives blood.
7. Donor rests and takes refreshments for at least 15 minutes.

This page should use clear visuals and short wording.

## Apheresis Donation Page

The official site explains apheresis donation.

Our page should explain:

- Apheresis collects selected blood components.
- The machine separates blood components.
- The needed component is collected.
- The remaining blood is returned to the donor.
- Staff monitor the donor during the process.
- It can collect platelets, plasma, or red blood cells.

Basic apheresis requirements:

- Age 18 to 65.
- Good health.
- Weight at least 50 kg.
- Extra checks may be required depending on the component being donated.
